# The logbook's bestiary: name, what it does, how to beat it.
beast.sweeper = Sweeper
beast.sweeper.text = A cleaning robot of the Academy that now sweeps away anything that moves. Walks to the edge, turns, walks back.
beast.sweeper.weak = Any shot. Or just jump over it.
